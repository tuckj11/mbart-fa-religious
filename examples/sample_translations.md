# Sample Translations

A handful of example outputs from the fine-tuned model, generated with beam search (4 beams).

| English | Persian (model output) |
|---|---|
| God said, let there be light | خدا گفت، بگذار نور باشد |
| God is the Father of our spirits | خدا پدر روح ما است |
| After baptism, we covenant to share the Gospel | پس از تعمید، ما پیمان می‌بندیم که مژده را به اشتراک بگذاریم |
| I so testify in the sacred name of Jesus Christ, amen | من چنین گواهی می‌دهم بنام مقدّس عیسی مسیح، آمین |

These reflect the kind of doctrinal/devotional phrasing the model was fine-tuned on — a general-purpose translator would be less likely to consistently produce the register and vocabulary conventions (e.g. "گواهی می‌دهم" for formal testimony, "مژده" for Gospel/glad tidings) seen here.
